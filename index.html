Rules go first

Ask for “the lease I signed in March”, “a photo of the beach” or “the call about pricing”. Documents, pictures, recordings and videos are searched by what they say, show and sound like. EmbeddingGemma 2 listens to the audio, on your Mac. Part of Pro.

Finds anything you describe

Ask for it in your own words.

Type what you remember: what a file says, what a picture shows, what someone said in a recording. PigeonHole finds it on your Mac, and one click opens it. With Pro, from any app: press ⌥ Space.

  1. Download itPigeonHole for Mac, for Apple Silicon.
  2. Drag it into ApplicationsOpen the file you downloaded and drag PigeonHole onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open itFrom Applications, like any other app. It's signed and checked by Apple, so it simply opens.

PigeonHole takes its name from the dovecote. Before the word meant a slot in a writing desk or a post office sorting frame, a PigeonHole was a nesting hole, one for every bird, and each bird flew home to its own. This one is for files. It is a Mac app, made in 2026 by Seymur Mammadov.

The paintings and prints on this page are public domain, from the Art Institute of Chicago. The drop capital is Farmhouse with Dovecotes by Claes Jansz. Visscher, 1620.
